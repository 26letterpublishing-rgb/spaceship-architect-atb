  grid can hold, its overflow remains in that original recovery snapshot.

  margins. Normal hex proportions are preserved. Regions retain their prior
  default framing and extended zoom-out.

  for all three sizes. Camera changes reuse existing SVG cells and are limited
  to one update per animation frame. Large grids now render 4,800 cells instead
  of the previous 5,760; Small renders 1,200 and Moon 300.
- Galaxy Map uses the generated white star with gently staggered CSS twinkling.
  Reduced-motion disables the animation. The original Sun artwork remains in
  solar-system maps. This applies to GM and PC views.
- Planet-directory selections close the directory before opening their map.
- Campaign Help and the feature registry describe the new sizes and boundaries.
  Script Help was preserved unchanged.
