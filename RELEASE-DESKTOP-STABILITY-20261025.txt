CONFIRMED LOCAL REPAIRS

1. Encounter preparation actions covered the editable arena.
   Reproduction: open GM Combat preparation and scroll to the Surface Arena at 1920x1080, 1366x768 or 1280x720. The sticky action bar intersected the map at all three sizes (at 1280, footer y=652..712; arena y=2..720).
   Cause: shared .encounter-preparation-actions position:sticky/bottom:8px in ui-reputation-pass.css.
   Repair: normal document flow on desktop widths >=1000px. Both preparation modes share this action bar; no control was removed. Save Preparation remains reachable by scrolling and actually returns to Campaign. Browser assertions cover map non-overlap and unobstructed hit-testing at all three dimensions. GM Combat Help now explains where to find these actions. Existing Help screenshot crops show other controls and were retained; new before/after screenshots are in the evidence directory.

2. Paint Terrain and Undo rebuilt the full planet surface twice per successful operation.
   Cause: the successful send already renders returned campaign state; each handler then called c.draw again solely to refresh Undo availability.
   Repair in planet-surfaces-ui.js: update the current Undo button after recording/removing its history, without the second reconstruction. This also applies to Apply to all. Error redraw paths, terrain data, visibility, camera behavior and server validation are unchanged.
   Real mouse stroke changed 54 hexes, then Undo restored every changed terrain value. Baseline paint had two render calls (65.1 and 62.7 ms); Undo added two (55.3 and 49.1 ms). Final verification: paint one call (58.3 ms), Undo one additional call (49.1 ms); both requests returned HTTP 200. This removes duplicate work, not all surface lag. These are individual local runs, not a statistical speedup estimate. Apply to all shares the repaired path but was not separately completed in this browser run.

3. Empty Probe Launcher console threw while plotting.
   Cause in probe-console.js: pending?.probeId === p?.id was true when both were undefined, then pending.kind was accessed.
   Repair: require both pending order and selected probe before comparing their IDs. Reproduced during the console sweep; all 54 representative console families now open without page errors. No console CSS/layout was changed. Computed styles match with the generic popup stylesheet enabled and disabled; consoles do not acquire its green vector frame. Ordinary card popups retain their frame, and closing a nested console returns to the open card inspector.
