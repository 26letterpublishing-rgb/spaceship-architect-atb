DRAMA CARD AUTOMATION AUDIT — October 16, 2026

Scope: all 72 cards in the app catalog. Every card already supports shared-deck ownership, viewing, playing/revealing and discard bookkeeping. Those generic controls are not counted as effect automation. The categories below describe effect automation.

This pass automates the seven low-risk bookkeeping effects listed first. None of the remaining effects are represented as implemented. Narrative effects should remain GM-led; supporting buttons may still be useful. Clear printed mechanics are listed separately from actual open questions. No dice are silently rolled.

AUTOMATED NOW (7 cards; 2 have narrative portions left to the GM)

06. LEVEL UP
    Doubles one selected, unclaimed XP award for this PC; claim it through the normal Inbox. Already-claimed awards are not rewritten.

32. DIFFERENT WAY OF DOING THINGS
    Adds 3 Reverence (maximum 10), or draws 2 cards after confirmation that the GM-approved alternate check was completed.

43. OH, NOW I REMEMBER
    Permanently adds +2.0 to a selected skill rated 0.9 or lower, after the player confirms the check resolved. No XP is charged.

47. SHARE THE WEALTH
    Deals one card to every campaign PC, including offline PCs.

61. BANK ERROR IN YOUR FAVOR
    Doubles a selected, unclaimed personal Credit award for this PC. Other recipients keep their original award.

70. VISION
    PARTIAL: discards the hand after confirmation that the GM established the rewind. Story/world state is not rolled back automatically.

71. MASSIVE PLOT TWIST
    PARTIAL: discards the hand and permanently removes this card from this campaign deck after confirmation that the GM established the story change.

AUTOMATION NEEDS CLARIFICATION OR AN AGREED INTERRUPT FLOW (28 cards)

01. PRACTICE ROLL
    A completed roll may already have spent resources or dealt damage. Define whether replacement rewinds only the score, or also all consequences.

04. DRAMATIC SAVE
    Define a reaction window before damage, and what happens when the interceptor cannot reach an adjacent legal square.

07. SONIC SPEED
    Define how an out-of-turn move or automatic Dodge interrupts an already open action/roll.

12. DON’T LOOK
    Define a pre-damage reaction window. The damage and radius doubling are otherwise clear.

14. REVENGEANCE
    Define the reaction window for capturing HP damage, and whether the next damage includes area damage or damage dealt through a starship.

19. BLIND SPOT
    Define which detection events qualify (narrative NPC spotting, sensors, or both), and how the free move interrupts the clock.

20. WEAK POINT
    Define whether the card modifies pending damage or retrospectively repairs an already resolved hit; also define whether enemy includes ships for turn suppression.

25. MALFUNCTION
    The GM must choose ammo depletion or recovery delay and its duration. Define when an already selected attack may be cancelled.

27. LONG SHOT
    Define whether a completed critical failure can be reversed after its consequences have applied.

30. ENOUGH!
    Immediate pause is clear. The GM must decide whether combat ends or resumes; define the approval flow.

34. FTL ESCAPE
    Clarify whether this also skips campaign navigation activation/partial travel, or only an encounter FTL delayed resolution.

35. NEVER MISS
    Define the latest point a miss can be replaced, and whether ship shield protection remains independent of ignored Damage Reduction.

39. NEAR MISS
    Needs a damage interception window before HP application; the replacement 1D6 stays a normal player roll.

45. CRITICAL MOMENT
    Define how late an NPC check can be replaced and whether already applied consequences are reversed.

46. BULLET TIME
    Needs an agreed interrupt/resume model for open dice dialogs, pending damage and concurrent ship operations.

51. POWERFLUX
    Identify eligible operation meters, including fixed-second timers without an existing Speed field, and whether it affects a single operation only.

52. REFLECT DAMAGE
    Define which entity takes reflected ship damage, whether shields absorb it, and the reaction window before the original damage applies.

55. GOOD ADVICE
    Define how the chosen next check is identified, including offline PCs or an already open roll dialog.

56. DOUBLING SEASON
    GM explicitly chooses doubling numeric values versus resolving twice. Define exclusions and interaction with permanent deck removal, rewinds and automatic plays.

57. FIRST SIGHT
    The GM must track the affected NPC and decide when fascination ends. Charisma success can then be automated against that approved target.

59. SPEAK FRIEND AND YOU MAY ENTER
    Clarify whether this bypasses the hacking minigame, the underlying Hacking check, or only a narrative password guess.

60. FATE POINT
    Define which five actions qualify and how delayed actions, free/fast actions, attacks and interruption interact. Ship operations are already excluded by the card.

62. PHOENIX
    Needs a before-next-turn rescue window and a rule for characters already removed by defeat cleanup.

63. GO FISH
    Choose how offline players contribute cards, how long the secret choice phase waits, and whether the GM can finish for absent players.

65. SCRY 3
    Choose who acts for offline draw recipients and how simultaneous draws are queued. Bottom-card order must remain private.

66. LIMIT BREAKER
    Needs a death-interception window and rules for resuming interrupted actions. The next-five-turn accounting is otherwise clear.

67. LOG JITSU
    GM must choose restored HP and legal position and decide which destruction consequences are reversed. Shield reset can be bookkeeping after that decision.

68. WHEEL OF FORTUNE
    Choose how offline players decide, whether a newly drawn Wheel interrupts another card deal, and whether the GM can resolve absent-player choices.

RECOMMENDED DECISIONS FOR THE NEXT AUTOMATION PASS

1. Reaction cards: add an explicit reaction opportunity before a result commits, freeze active combat time while it is open, and retain GM override. Avoid silently rolling back already committed damage, inventory or world changes. Decide whether this window is always offered or opened only when a relevant card is held.
2. Offline card choices (Go Fish, Scry 3, Wheel of Fortune): allow the GM to resolve for absent players; do not silently discard their hands. Confirm your preferred default.
3. Doubling Season: let the GM select the original card and approve the exact doubled effect before anything changes.
4. Narrative cards: keep visible play/discard, optional GM notes and ordinary adjustment tools; no automatic story authorship.
