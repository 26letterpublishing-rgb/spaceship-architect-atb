same dice/result for Retry Submit. A completed or superseded request clears
instead of trapping the player in a retry. Required character and starship rolls
cannot be dismissed through X or Escape before completion.

preserves form actions, restores buttons that change purpose between stages,

Defense, First Aid and starship recovery. GM damage-count/scatter takeover keeps
ownership of the follow-up. Obsolete player prompts and active animations clear.
Oxygen and Cleanser operator rolls are protected; Cleanser spectators can return.
