Requested behavior
- Star System maps default to Edit Planets. Clicking a planet opens the shared editor for name, size, public description and appearance. Browse Planets exposes the same editor.
- Exclusive highlighted modes distinguish editing, panning, moving ships, timed travel and repositioning planets. Galaxy, surface/region, reusable space-object editors and combat preparation use explicit modes appropriate to their operations. Pending object placement has its own highlighted action; Cancel returns to the previous mode.
- The ship selector appears in the GM movement/travel modes. Repositioning a planet carries its orbit coordinates without changing surface hex locations. Server authorization and combat restrictions remain enforced.
- Shared GM/PC system planet art scales to Large 110%, Small 85%, Moon 60% of the previous icon size. Existing art is reused.
- The location hierarchy remains at the left in every Campaign map view, with no separate location heading shifting it. Four equal sections retain blank levels, wrapped names, active-level glow and the small party pin. Names use 22/20/18/16px from left to right and are centered within the sloping segments.
- Paint Terrain offers Apply to all for Temperate, Desert, Ice, Volcanic, Ocean and Moon. The confirmation names the selected terrain. The atomic fill affects the current planet or region, preserves cities/regions/descriptions/attachments, and supports Undo Stroke. City brushes do not offer the fill action.
- Planet shrinking first crops unused surroundings, then removes unused coordinate gaps while retaining occupied-axis order. If relocation or loss is unavoidable, saving requires confirmation of: Some Cities and Regions may be lost when reducing the size of planets.
- Linked party, ship, character, navigation and journey locations are remapped. Even an over-capacity confirmed shrink keeps travelers on valid hexes. The previous world and locations are retained in a private recovery snapshot; snapshots remain excluded from live GM/PC payloads. Stale edits from an older planet size are rejected.
- Campaign and combat-preparation Help describes the actual modes. Four Campaign captures were refreshed, with arrows aligned to the current controls. Script Help content remains unchanged by this pass.
