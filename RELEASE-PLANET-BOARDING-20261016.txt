Implemented

- View System is beside Edit Solar System. Shops has a compact action row; narrow map layouts stack without overlapping Shops.
- New Explore sessions start the party on Harbor, five hexes from parked Protos. Party and ship positions are independent and persist in campaign saves.
- Ashore PCs disappear from the interior, with movement and console controls disabled. Crew assignments remain; the header distinguishes crew from people aboard.
- Moving the party onto the ship boards PCs at available airlock cells or nearest legal interior spaces. Repeating the placement preserves existing aboard locations. Moving away clears stations and walking routes.
- Departure requires returning to the ship, working launch equipment and a stationed Bridge character.
- Landed interiors reuse the landing hex terrain pattern without planetary hex outlines. Space returns after departure. No new raster assets.
- Help explains editing, boarding and departure. Secondary crew assignments no longer hide a character's actual boarding location.
