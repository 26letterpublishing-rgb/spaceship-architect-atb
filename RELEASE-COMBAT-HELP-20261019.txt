Delay Rate, with Higher rates finish sooner. No rates, factors, critical

in this Help pass. The prior authorized combat-submission/recovery fixes are
included in this release; see RELEASE-SUBMISSION-RECOVERY-20261019.txt.

Corrections to the handoff:
- Restore Latest and Choose Earlier Checkpoint are the current recovery names.
- Pause Everything is normally required before opening Delay; a paused NPC turn
  also allows it. Queued setup requires resolution/Pass before its effect starts.
- Naming a custom delay does not perform a scan or deal damage automatically.
- Object placement is Starship preparation; Surface uses floor/wall/door tools.
  Boundaries use two adjacent floor squares. Live surface fog has its own preview
  and reveal controls. Preparation uses Expand Zone and Terrain controls.
- Ship selection determines initial vessel assignment; the GM checks interior
  positions before engaging the clock. Assignment does not imply a station.
- Arena exports are reusable setups, not live battle recovery files.
- Explicit submission, retries, GM takeover, Reset/Undo/Clear and End Combat are
  explained using the current workflow and control labels.
