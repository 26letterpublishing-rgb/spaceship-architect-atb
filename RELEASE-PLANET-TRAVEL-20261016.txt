October 16, 2026 — Campaign travel, map editing, character UI and queued local release

Scope
Includes the previously authorized local planet/boarding, mail, galaxy-layout and queued UI passes, plus the follow-up requests in this conversation. Help expansion is the next pass; this release only updates existing short help references affected by renamed/moved controls.

Delivered
- Airlocks prefer every usable unoccupied hull square, with the largest SIC as fallback. Bridge departure and the gold Console View launcher/toggle share actual station availability. Boarding re-enables movement without requiring a reload.
- Planet maps have 1,200 hexes, periodic east/west terrain and horizontal wrapping. Only one set of 1,200 hex elements is authored; SVG use references provide seam copies. Six shared terrain patterns replace per-hex image loading, and seeded terrain is cached. Painting, city/erase-city brushes, concurrent-safe undo, marker dragging, region navigation and public/private location editors are visible to the GM. New regions inherit their parent hex terrain uniformly.
- Campaign maps fill the available viewport; Enlarge Map uses the full frame. Detail actions stay alongside the map. Colored hierarchy buttons and a player-view indicator distinguish GM browsing from the shared view.
- Players request Travel Here; the GM approves/declines in the mail window. Timed party journeys use the shortest wrapped route and distance-based speed (2 seconds per region hex, 4 per surface hex; capped at 120 seconds). Movement syncs across clients and survives a server restart. GM approval brings participants to Campaign except players shopping, advancing, rolling or editing ships. GM relocation from a broader map requires confirmation, boards the party and seats a conscious PC at an available Bridge station.
- Departure returns the initiating player from the console to Campaign. Hover departure glows and scales over the first four seconds; Aerofoil departure fires the rear engines and leaves north. Orbit uses original generated planet artwork and a 50-second oval path. Final landing eases rapidly down to a slow finish over four seconds. Flight transitions use the orbit position to avoid a jump when entering/leaving its track. Status says Exiting atmosphere. Campaign controls use mute-aware synthesized UI sounds.
- Star details offer Enter Star System. Previously visited planets expose their original graphic, public description and compact read-only surface preview, while GM notes remain private.
- GM and PC mail/settings icons match. Mail is a separate window and shops have their own tab. GM shop creation/editing is inline. Prompt/Give places the roll request across the top and the other groups side by side. Crew Log opens from the PC header. The drawer has six tabs in one row and functional weapon dropdowns. Duplicate advancement EXP is hidden. Race portraits fill the scrolling view and keep heads visible. Creation guidance remains active until the relevant task is complete, including Back/re-render and subtype steps.
- Twelve 512x512 subrace sprite atlases use the established two-variant, 840ms walking system: four Android, four Antropic and four Yuhorn Symitron types. Draco Prime excludes Kabuto, Sked'er and Xithx (and retains the existing Spiddix exclusion). New atlas payload is approximately 903 KB.
