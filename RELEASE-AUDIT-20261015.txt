Spaceship Architect update and verification — October 15, 2026

STORAGE BLOCKER
The campaign-creation failure was a localStorage quota exception while saving the
new GM token. Full campaign snapshots competed with small login/settings records.
Character initialization and draft saves also assumed localStorage always worked.

browser-storage.js now archives campaign cache/backup values in IndexedDB, deleting
an old localStorage entry only after its database transaction commits. Other saves
fall back to the same durable database if localStorage is full. No unrelated data
is evicted. Room/GM/character initialization waits for existing archived saves;
room creation, loading and character navigation wait for durable credential writes.
Character save status reports completion only after the write finishes. Failed
writes retain the pending work, show an error and guard against leaving the page.
Optional session hints/tab memory cannot interrupt character creation or GM tabs.
A genuinely full/disabled IndexedDB still requires export; the app must not claim
that a failed local save succeeded.

Browser tests on a disposable local origin:
- Filled localStorage to its quota, retaining an existing character and 1.5 MB
  campaign backup. Main menu and new campaign opened successfully.
- Verified the archived backup and original character were preserved.
- Filled BOTH localStorage and sessionStorage, without adding removable caches.
  Created Quota Survivor, changed its name, saw Saved locally, reloaded and
  verified its draft/name and the original character remained.
- Created another campaign with both stores full, reloaded the GM page,
  switched to Characters, and returned to Main Menu without resetting the room.
- Unit regressions cover migration, overflow with both stores full, reload,
  replacement of stale values, deletion, rapid writes and failed transactions.

WEAPON RULES IMPLEMENTED
- Repeated Aim: up to four Perception dice. Preserve the app's 2D4 plus all
  Strength unarmed damage and range adjustment to Reaction Defense.
- Fire: each eight active combat seconds; pauses freeze it; queued damage,
  intensity growth, extinguishing and Stop/Drop/Roll. Other printed six-second
  weapon effects remain six seconds. Wet lasts to encounter end.
- Kevlar: 20 Physical reduction. Space Suit: depleting protection and HP overflow.
- Grenade placement/scatter and fuses; held/cooked explosives, Sticky detachment,
  proximity triggers, cover blocking blast/Flash effects, Mini Nuke 30-unit blast
  and fallout, and automatic nearby-wall counting for Rubber Bouncy Bombs.
- Hull Bore: moving destructible drone and 18-second drilling to an actual opening;
  Skull Bore: moving/attached drone and six-second escalating Escape checks.
- Force Bazooka: half-damage line up to 10 units, stopped by walls.
  Amplify knockback: collision stops motion and adds 2D10.
- Charged weapon choices, Gravity rating, Solar light, reflected Phazor option,
  LX burst, Murasama sheathing, Bizi two-target split, Thunder/Greentip auxiliary
  dice, Homing/Skull charge locks, electricity and cold/status interactions.
- Ammunition counts, purchased reload supplies, MK-U GM programming and server
  validation; preserve ammunition/programming through campaign saves.
- Thrown melee attack/range, half damage before armor, direction-dependent
  movement bonus and retrieval before the weapon can be used again.

Remaining explicit decision: Silent Scope does not specify how many rounds one
Iron/Magnesium unit provides. The GM can configure loaded rounds manually;
automatic mineral-to-round conversion awaits the user's number.
Remaining limitations for a subsequent rules/playtest pass: size-based Net Gun
Escape adjustment is not automated; Rubber Bouncy Bomb multi-target wall/accuracy
interpretation needs playtest review; removal of an explosive-owning NPC before
its timer resolves needs a dedicated regression. These are not declared complete.
The original core-rulebook audit remains a historical baseline; this report
supersedes its statements that the above features are entirely unimplemented.

Intentionally excluded/unchanged: Arm Cannon (weapon PDF page 50 onward), warp
combat/bubble merging/FTL mines, Reverse Targeting ID, Separation Module, custom
SIC building, multiple floors, angled/offset squares and Wired Downgrade.
Connector Cable remains undecided. SA2E differences are reference notes, not
permission to replace the approved app rules with the draft's rules.
