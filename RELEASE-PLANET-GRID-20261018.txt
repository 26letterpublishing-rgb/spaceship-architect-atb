Result
Planet surfaces now contain 144 columns x 40 rows (5,760 unique cells).
Fit Map / maximum zoom-out shows one circumference. The left and right
edges join; horizontal panning moves that seam without repeating the world
inside the overview. All rows remain visible, with normal hex proportions.
The shared implementation serves both GM and PC maps and surface previews.
Region maps remain 15 x 10, with their prior default and extended zoom range.

Data and travel
Legacy IDs 0–1199 retain their original 30-column coordinates and generated
terrain. New IDs 1200–5759 address an additional 114-column band. Existing
authored descriptions, cities, secrets, attachments, regions, starts, party
and ship locations therefore require no destructive save migration.
Added continents are seeded uniquely and meet the original shores. Authored
overrides still win. Both sides use the same coordinates, hit testing and
route geometry. Travel wraps at the new outer seam. Previously saved journey
endpoints/progress are retained and their display route uses current geometry.
Normalization, painting limits, new regions, portable maps, campaign saves
and adventure starts accept the expanded IDs. Regional IDs are unchanged.

Processing
Seeded terrain and immutable cell geometry are cached. Camera changes reuse
the existing SVG world and lightweight wrapped instances. Pan/wheel changes
are coalesced to one view update per animation frame; they do not fetch,
regenerate or rebuild individual hexes. The larger map creates more SVG nodes
on initial render; no frame-rate claim or device-wide benchmark is made.

Geometry limitation
A fixed world with undistorted hexes cannot fill every possible viewport
aspect ratio in both directions. Fit Map retains the whole world and adds
vertical margins on taller screens or side margins on very wide/short screens.
The tested desktop overview fills the width; panning still joins its edges.
World dimensions never change when a window is resized or another PC joins.
